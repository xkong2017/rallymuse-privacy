RallyMuse Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Applies to the RallyMuse iOS app

Short version: RallyMuse analyzes your tennis videos entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data, videos, or analysis results. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no trackers.

1. Data we do not collect

RallyMuse does not collect or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party. Specifically, RallyMuse never sends us:

  • Your videos or recordings;
  • Analysis results, statistics, or coaching reports;
  • Your name, email address, or contact information;
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location;
  • Any crash logs, usage metrics, or telemetry.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything RallyMuse creates — imported videos, analysis results, calibration profiles, reels, and settings — is stored locally in the app's storage on your iPhone and remains there until you delete it (in the app's Settings, by deleting sessions, or by uninstalling the app). Deleting the app removes all of this data.

3. Camera, microphone, and photo library

RallyMuse asks for these permissions only when you use the matching feature, and only to provide that feature:

  • Camera & microphone — to record match video from the fence mount. Recordings are saved only to your device.
  • Photo library — to import a video you pick, and to save exported clips or frame captures that you explicitly choose to save.

Videos are never uploaded anywhere by RallyMuse.

4. Local network access

Optionally, RallyMuse can discover a RallyMuse-compatible assistant running on your own computer on your home Wi-Fi network (via Apple's Bonjour). This connection is directly between your iPhone and your own computer on your local network. No data passes through our servers — we operate none for this feature — and the feature is entirely optional. If you never use it, nothing ever leaves your device.

5. Reminders

If you choose to add a prescribed drill to Apple Reminders, RallyMuse writes that reminder through Apple's Reminders framework at your request. We never read or collect your reminders.

6. Children

RallyMuse does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

7. GDPR / CCPA

Because RallyMuse does not collect, process, or transfer personal data off the device, there is no personal data for us to process on your behalf or share with any party.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of RallyMuse introduces any data collection, this policy will be updated and the change disclosed in the app before that feature is enabled.

9. Contact

Questions or support: support@f3tennis.com
