CourtMuse Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026 · Applies to the CourtMuse iOS app

Short version: your match video is analysed entirely on your iPhone and is never uploaded. Coaching runs on your device too, using Apple Intelligence, on iOS 26 and later — that is free and sends nothing. On older iPhones the coach falls back to a cloud model with a daily question allowance, and in that case only match measurements and your typed question are sent — never video. Cloud coaching is off until you turn it on.

1. What is processed, and where

WhatWhere it is processedDoes it leave your iPhone?
Match video, rally cuts, exported reels On your iPhone No. Never uploaded, to us or anyone.
Analysis: strokes, speeds, bounce positions, calibration, scores On your iPhone No, except as part of coaching below.
Coaching on iOS 26+ (Apple Intelligence) On your iPhone, using Apple's on-device model No. Free and unlimited; nothing is sent.

2. Cloud coaching (optional, older iPhones only)

On iOS 26 and later, coaching runs on your device and this section does not apply. On older iPhones the on-device model is not available, so CourtMuse offers a cloud coach instead. It is off by default. You turn it on in Settings, and you may turn it off again at any time.

When it is on and you ask the coach a question, we send:

  • your typed question;
  • a compact summary of this match's measurements — shot types, spin, speeds, and where each ball bounced and was struck;
  • the player and opponent names you entered, if any.

We do not send your video, your photo library, your name or email address, your contacts, your location, or any advertising identifier. The relay holds the model provider's credentials, so no API key is stored in the app.

To count questions against the daily allowance, we store an opaque device identifier generated by the app on first use. It contains no personal information, is not your Apple ID, is not the advertising identifier, and is deleted when the counting window expires. It is used for nothing except the allowance.

3. Daily allowances

Cloud coaching costs us money per question, so it is metered. Free accounts get a small number of questions per day; a CourtMuse Coach subscription raises the allowance. On iOS 26+ there is no allowance at all, because the coach runs on your own hardware.

Subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your card details, billing address, or Apple ID. We receive only whether a subscription is currently active, so we know which allowance to apply.

4. Data stored on your device

Everything CourtMuse creates — imported videos, analysis results, calibration profiles, reels, and settings — is stored locally in the app's storage on your iPhone and remains there until you delete it (in the app's Settings, by deleting sessions, or by uninstalling the app). Deleting the app removes all of this data.

5. Camera, microphone, and photo library

CourtMuse asks for these permissions only when you use the matching feature, and only to provide that feature:

  • Camera & microphone — to record match video from the fence mount. Recordings are saved only to your device.
  • Photo library — to import a video you pick, and to save exported clips or frame captures that you explicitly choose to save.

Videos are never uploaded anywhere by CourtMuse, including by the cloud coach.

6. Local network access

Optionally, CourtMuse can discover a CourtMuse-compatible assistant running on your own computer on your home Wi-Fi network (via Apple's Bonjour). This connection is directly between your iPhone and your own computer on your local network. No data passes through our servers for this feature, and it is entirely optional. If you never use it, nothing is sent to your computer.

7. Reminders

If you choose to add a prescribed drill to Apple Reminders, CourtMuse writes that reminder through Apple's Reminders framework at your request. We never read or collect your reminders.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not upload your match video, ever.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your video or your match data to train models.
  • We do not run advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.

9. Children

CourtMuse is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. GDPR / CCPA

For players who never enable cloud coaching, CourtMuse processes no personal data off the device, so there is nothing for us to process on your behalf.

If you enable cloud coaching, the match summary and your question are processed to answer you, on the basis of your consent (which you give by switching the feature on and can withdraw by switching it off). We keep quota counters, which contain no personal information, only for the current allowance window. We do not sell personal information. To request access to or deletion of anything we hold, email us.

11. Changes to this policy

If a future version of CourtMuse introduces new data collection, this policy will be updated and the change disclosed in the app before that feature is enabled. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
